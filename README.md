# Marvellous Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Marvellous Aircon Pte Ltd provides aircon servicing, cleaning, repairs, maintenance, and installation for homes and businesses in Singapore. The proposed homepage would help visitors choose the right service, understand the total price, and book online.

## Main Improvements

1. **Explain what customers can book upfront.** Replace broad promotional claims in the hero with a concrete service proposition for customers who need routine maintenance, deeper cleaning, a repair, or a new installation.
2. **Show unit rates and payable totals together.** Headline per-fan-coil rates can be mistaken for single-unit prices. Pricing tables would show the unit quantity, applicable BTU range, per-unit rate, total price, and package conditions together so visitors can compare options.
3. **Give each service a direct route to booking.** Consolidate repeated gas top-up content and group routine servicing, chemical and steam cleaning, repair and troubleshooting, installation, and maintenance packages by customer need. Pair each service group with a relevant booking action.

## Design Improvements

A focused hero would introduce the main services. Consistent service cards, aligned pricing tables, and booking buttons beside relevant details would make the page easier to scan on desktop and mobile.
